    Built for the data real estate actually holds.

    01Keep data in region

    Production design processes documents in an Australian AWS region, with no model training on your data.

    02Least privilege

    Agents start read-only. Anything that writes to a system goes through a person’s approval first.

    03Audit everything

    Every extraction, suggestion and edit is logged, so any answer can be traced back to its source page.

    04Measure honestly

    Accuracy is tested on a labelled set before launch and re-checked on every change, then reported to the team.

    These are the design principles for a production build. This public demo is a static page: it stores nothing and has no back end.

    Questions

    Before you try it.

    Are the numbers real?

    The three scenarios are fictional. Baselines come from the notes shown beside them, and every target is a proposal to be tested, not a result. In live mode the model is told to use only figures that appear in your notes.

    Does it store my notes?

    No. The page has no back end. In live mode your notes go from your browser straight to Anthropic’s API using your own key. Please keep real client data out of a demo.

    Why start with discovery instead of a chatbot?

    The request is rarely the problem. In the leasing sample, a tenant chatbot would have left forty hand-typed leases a week and a spreadsheet of critical dates untouched.

    What would a real engagement look like?

    Sit beside the team, map the work, agree a baseline and a measure, build and ship one thing, train the people who use it, then check the numbers. This tool is the first step of that loop.
